Guiding Our Community

MUBASA is led by a democratically elected Executive Committee supported by a dedicated Cabinet and advised by a Council of Elders. Together, they work tirelessly to ensure the association fulfills its mission and serves every member with integrity and commitment.

Executive Committee

The elected leaders steering MUBASA towards its mission and vision

John Bosco Byamukama

President

Final-year student of Bachelor of Commerce. John Bosco has been an active member of MUBASA since his first year and has served in various capacities before his election as President. His leadership focuses on unity, academic excellence, and cultural preservation.

Sarah Namara Tukamushaba

Vice President

Third-year student of Business Administration. Sarah brings organizational excellence and passion for community development to her role. She oversees internal affairs and coordinates between the executive and general membership.

Emmanuel Kwizera

General Secretary

Third-year student of Economics. Emmanuel manages the association's documentation, meeting records, and correspondence. His attention to detail ensures smooth administrative operations.

Peter Nshimye

Treasurer

Second-year student of Bachelor of Business Computing. Peter handles the association's finances with transparency and accountability, preparing budgets and financial reports for all activities.

Mary Goretti Turyagyenda

Speaker

Fourth-year student of International Business. Mary presides over general assembly meetings and ensures democratic participation in all association decisions.

Daniel Tumusiime

Minister of Information

Third-year student of Mass Communication. Daniel manages MUBASA's public relations, social media presence, and communications strategy.

Elders Council

Wisdom and guidance from respected community elders

Mzee Sebastian Sebufiri

Chairman, Elders Council

A respected community leader with decades of experience in cultural matters. Mzee Sebufiri provides invaluable guidance to the executive committee on all matters concerning Bafumbira traditions and values.

Mama Prossy Nyirahabimana

Cultural Advisor

An expert in Bafumbira language and oral traditions. Mama Prossy mentors young members on the importance of preserving the Kifumbira language and ensures cultural events maintain authenticity.

Mzee Gregory Bwanbale

Youth Mentor

A retired educator dedicated to guiding the younger generation. Mzee Gregory offers life advice, academic encouragement, and moral support to all members of the association.

Be Part of Our Leadership

MUBASA leadership positions are open to all registered members through democratic elections held annually. If you are passionate about serving your community, we encourage you to get involved and make a difference.
